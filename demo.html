Demo FAQ

How long is the demo?
About 20 minutes. We keep it focused: a short intro, a live generation run on a real drawing, and time for your questions. No 40-slide deck.
What should I bring?
A real shop drawing, submittal, or spec sheet for a piece of equipment you actually model. PDF or image is fine. The more typical it is of your day-to-day work, the more useful the demo.
Is my drawing data confidential?
Yes. Anything you share for the demo is used only to run that session, is never used to train shared models, and can be deleted on request. We're happy to sign an NDA beforehand.
Do you offer a trial after?
Yes. Teams that want to keep going get a guided pilot in their own Revit and ACC environment, so you can validate APEX on your real projects before committing.

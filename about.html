Our story

We got tired of building families by hand.

APEX started where most good construction tools start — on a job, with a deadline, and a Revit library that was nowhere near ready. Our founders were detailers and VDC leads who spent more nights than they'll admit modeling families one parameter at a time.

Every project meant rebuilding the same content from scratch, then watching that hard-won model never quite make it to the field. The gap between what was coordinated in BIM and what actually got laid out, surveyed, and installed was where the hours — and the rework — disappeared.

So we built APEX to close it: automate the generation of construction-ready Revit families, and connect the model directly to layout and survey. Today we're in private beta with leading electrical contractors, building the tool we always wished we'd had on the trailer.
